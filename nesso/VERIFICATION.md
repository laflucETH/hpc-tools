## On Euler (GPU)

Full production run, 2026-11-17: **4,929 pairs submitted, 4,568 scored**, 3 refused as repeated
ligand entities, 358 without a sequence or a ligand. 8 shards on RTX 4090s, ~1.7-2.5 s per
pair. The run left scratch at 7,055 files, against 7,002 before it started, and put six files
in project storage. Peak memory per shard was 11.5 GB of the 24 GB requested, so **2 cores and
14 GB is the right request** for a shard of this shape -- the 24 GB was an over-correction from
the 4-core pilot, whose 15.95 GB was driven by its dataloader workers.
